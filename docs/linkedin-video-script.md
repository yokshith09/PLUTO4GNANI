# Pluto Voice: LinkedIn demo video script

**Length:** about 75 seconds. **Format:** screen recording with your voice over it, 1080p, 16:9 (crop to 1:1 for the feed if you like).
**Tools:** OBS Studio or Xbox Game Bar (Win+Alt+R), a quiet room, a headset mic. Edit in CapCut or Clipchamp; add captions (most people watch muted).

## Before you press record
- Make a clean demo account state: a few sample tasks, one finished sample meeting with a summary.
- Cloud voice ON, keys saved, always-on listening ON, floating pet visible.
- Close other windows and notifications. Open Notepad ready for the dictation scene.
- Do each scene as a separate take. Short clips are easier to cut together.

## Tips
- Start scene 2 with the real voice, not a silent screen: the first 3 seconds decide if people keep watching.
- Speak a little slower than normal; Pluto and viewers both benefit.
- If a take fails live, cut it; do not explain the failure.
- Add soft background music at low volume and keep your voice clear.
